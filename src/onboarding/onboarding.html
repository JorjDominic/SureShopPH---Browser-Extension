How it works

1

Supported sites

SureShop checks Shopee, Lazada, and FB Marketplace product pages for risk signals. URLs from other supported shopping sites are checked automatically.

Shopee Lazada FB Marketplace
2

Run a scan

Open the SureShop side panel and press Quick Scan, Deep Scan, or Scan Comments. We never scan without your action.

Press Alt+Shift+S to open SureShop instantly on any page.
3

Your privacy

We only send visible product details — name, price, ratings, public reviews, seller info — for analysis. We never send your account info, login state, or payment details.

How the risk score works

Each scan combines seller age, response rate, rating patterns, review signals, and price anomalies into a single 0–100 score. Lower is safer.

Low Risk  0–39

Looks normal, common patterns

Medium Risk  40–69

Some warning signs, review carefully

High Risk  70–100

Strong scam indicators, avoid if possible

SureShop is an assistant, not a guarantee. Always use your own judgment before purchasing.
