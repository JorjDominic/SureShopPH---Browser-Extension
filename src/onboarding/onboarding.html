We scan supported sites

SureShop checks Shopee, Lazada, and FB Marketplace product pages for risk signals. URLs of other shopping sites we support are checked automatically.

2

Click the icon, run a scan

Open the SureShop side panel and press Normal Scan, Deep Scan, or Scan Comments. We never run a scan without your action.

Tip: press Alt+Shift+S to open SureShop on any page.

3

Your privacy

We send the visible product details (name, price, ratings, public reviews, seller info) to the SureShop service for analysis. We never send your account, login state, payment details, or browsing history.

You can clear all stored history and feedback any time in Settings.

How the risk score works

Each scan combines several signals — seller age, response rate, rating distribution, review patterns, price anomalies, and language signals in comments — into a single 0–100 score. Lower is safer.

  • 0–39 Low risk — looks normal, common patterns
  • 40–69 Medium risk — some warning signs, review carefully
  • 70–100 High risk — strong scam indicators, avoid if possible

SureShop is an assistant, not a guarantee. Use your own judgment before purchasing.
